Category comparisons

Every fund in a category over its longest common history, against the index it is measured by. Computed as of {{ as_of }}.

One row per category. Each is computed for both plans where both exist; the count and the link are the direct plan, which is what a self-directed investor buys.

Active share is the median actively managed fund's distance from its index today; selection is the median fund's return beyond a passive large, mid and small cap mix over the last ten years, after fees. Blank where a category has no index fund to weigh against or no cap-index regression makes sense.
