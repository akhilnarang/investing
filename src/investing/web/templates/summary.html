Reading the table

  • Each category is measured against the benchmark its scheme documents name, or the nearest series that is publicly served. {% for g, names in benches.items() if names %}{{ g }}: {{ names|join(', ') }}{{ '; ' if not loop.last else '.' }}{% endfor %}
  • A median hides the spread. A category can lead at the median while its worst fund lags by five points a year; the category pages show the whole distribution.
  • Survivorship bias runs the same way in every row: merged and wound-up funds are gone, so every category looks a little better than it was.

What the columns cannot tell you

  • Which fund to pick. The category medians differ by less than the funds within them do.
  • Whether the lead survives. Small and mid cap categories carry the mid-cap tilt of the period, debt categories the rate cycle, commodities the rupee; the category pages separate what they can.
  • Past returns; not investment advice.
